How to use
Map 0 is the seed triangle. Every other map is a linear map applied to it, shown as a coloured outline. The drawing applies all maps over and over, down to the chosen depth.

Select a map by tapping its outline, drag inside it to move it. On the selected frame: the two square corners scale proportionally (Shift: free), the two curved corners rotate about the centre (Shift: snap to 15°), the bars on the top and right edges stretch in one direction, and the slanted handles on the bottom and left edges shear along that edge.

Drag empty space to pan, use the wheel or pinch to zoom. Arrow keys nudge the selected map, Delete removes it. The link in the address bar encodes the whole figure.
